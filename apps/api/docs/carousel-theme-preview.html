A PRACTICAL PLAYBOOK

Build a habit that survives busy weeks.

Small systems create durable momentum.

Consistency is designed

The goal is not more motivation. It is a workflow with fewer decisions, a clear trigger, and a next step that feels easy to start.

Three moves that compound

  • Choose one repeatable cue.
  • Make the first action tiny.
  • Review the system every Friday.
You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.
